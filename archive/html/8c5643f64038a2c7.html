Philosophical Perspectives on Needs

How we fundamentally view “needs” is a critical foundation for how they influence our decisions and guide our assessments.  The following resources offer a glimpse into the research and conversations on the topic within a variety of disciplines. 


Note: Special thanks to Jolanta Kavale for initially developing this resource page.
